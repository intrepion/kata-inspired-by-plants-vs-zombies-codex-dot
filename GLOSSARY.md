# Harbor language

- **Light**: Spendable energy collected from floating motes and Wicks.
- **Keeper**: A placeable harbor defender: Wick, Bell, Anchor or Frost.
- **Procession**: One scheduled wave of approaching sea spirits.
- **Breach**: A spirit crossing the left harbor boundary. Three breaches lose a chapter.
- **Chapter**: One map with three processions and a clean starting economy.
- **Tier**: A keeper's base or improved form; upgrading costs light.
