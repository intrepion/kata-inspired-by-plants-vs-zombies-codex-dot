# Lanterns at Low Tide

Design choices are delegated by the user. No interactive interview occurred. Grill with Docs was adapted into a settled decision tree: genre → original harbor identity → three chapters → four complementary keepers → deterministic simulation → explicit lifecycle and local save. TDD seams chosen under delegated authority: game commands/time advancement and save decoding. Reviews compare the implementation commit to the initial documentation commit.

A complete original five-lane, seven-column harbor defense. Collect floating light and collector output. Place Wick (income), Bell (ranged damage), Anchor (durable blocker), and Frost (slow, piercing armor). Occupied cells upgrade to tier two or salvage. Invalid actions are atomic; repeated placement cannot double spend; selection cancellation is free. Three chapters of three waves each, previewed lanes/enemy types, optional preparation and accelerated time, distinct runner/armored/drifter enemies and a Drowned Admiral finale. Three breaches lose a chapter; retry starts that chapter cleanly. Win advances once; completion remains saved and replayable. Save active state including enemies, timers and resources; reload paused. Corrupt/unsupported data resets safely. Hidden tab pauses. No offline progression.

Responsive pointer/touch and keyboard buttons, Escape cancellation, Space pause, generated vector character art and synthesized audio. Visible feedback for placement, affordability, upgrades and collection; tutorial and win/loss narrative. No paid dependencies or remote runtime services. Domain tests, typecheck, build, audit, independent standards/spec reviews, visible Mac Chrome gameplay and exact-final-commit CI required. Physical touch and listening are separate evidence boundaries.
